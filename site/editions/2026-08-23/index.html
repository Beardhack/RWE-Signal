Brain Researcher Made Agentic Neuroimaging Auditable

A Stanford-led harness raised correct first-tool selection from 23.3% to 93.6%, then bound real analyses to frozen plans, provenance, multiverse tests, and reviewable claim states.

RWE-Signal  ·  August 23, 2026  ·  Daily edition

A scientific agent is becoming more than a model that can run code. Brain Researcher treats the durable output as a governed research episode: the question, approved alternatives, tool calls, evidence, artifacts, checks, and final claim state remain connected in a record another scientist can reopen.

The new preprint tests that architecture across seven frontier models and on collaborator-led neuroimaging studies. It also arrives with an MIT-licensed agent kit, MCP adapters, deterministic review kernels, sanitized demos, and evaluation rubrics. A newly indexed behavioral-medicine study extends the edition's privacy thread: a local LLM turned sensitive trauma narratives into analyzable phenotypes without a cloud inference path.

The agent produced an evidence object

The August 20 Brain Researcher preprint describes a researcher-governed harness operating inside a neuroimaging workspace. A machine-readable tool registry and a provenance-linked knowledge graph guide routing; an MCP server mediates model actions; version-pinned executors run approved calls; and the audit bundle preserves the committed plan, logs, artifacts, consulted evidence, and the checks applied to each claim.

Across 60 tool-calling tasks and seven frontier models, correct first-action routing rose from 23.3% without the harness to 93.6% with it. Capability coverage increased from 49.8% to 94.5%, and handoff sufficiency from 47.4% to 76.1%. In a separate 50-question benchmark, the share of claims with locatable, supportive evidence rose from 4.6% to 22.0%.

The paired comparison measures the registry, graph, and constraint layer together rather than isolating one component, and the authors note that target curation may share vocabulary with the registry. Grounding is the clearest next unlock: a 4.8-fold improvement is meaningful, while 22.0% leaves ample engineering headroom. The contribution is unusually inspectable because the MIT-licensed v0.3.0 agent kit exposes instructions, MCP adapters, evaluation rubrics, deterministic review kernels, and redaction checks; Zenodo archives the release.

Multiverse analysis became part of the claim

Brain Researcher places two dated records around execution. Before results are observed, a commitment card fixes the question, allowed alternatives, success and failure criteria, and a detectable content hash. After execution, a claim card records scope, checks, and one of six states: accepted, qualified, revised, blocked, rejected, or deferred. Methodological authority remains with the researcher; the system makes each decision persistent.

Three collaborator-led studies show the record operating on real neuroimaging evidence. In an FBIRN schizophrenia cohort of 363 people, the harness expanded the analysis into a 480-specification multiverse and made estimator-dependent support visible. In the SUDMEX OpenNeuro cocaine-use-disorder cohort of 138 people, 36 specifications rejected all five prespecified connectivity-behavior associations; an exploratory 70-combination screen produced no FDR-surviving effect, so it was converted into a replication plan rather than promoted. A cross-cultural social-cognition analysis of 21 studies withheld a settled claim because its subgroups had only six to eight entries.

In the HCP episode, the system evaluated 116 candidate prediction pipelines and, after a selector audit, carried a frozen workflow into matched evaluation and four additional outcomes. A TRIBE foundation-model episode similarly turned exploration into a prospectively frozen sound-representation test using new stimulus panels and previously unused collections.

The preprint also documents the human checkpoint honestly. Automated review missed sign-blind scoring after a fallback to a general coding agent; a researcher found the error by inspecting code, outputs, and the specification curve, and the team added directionality and fallback-warning checks. That correction strengthens the infrastructure lesson: auditability gives expert review leverage and lets a discovered failure become a reusable rule.

A local LLM structured sensitive trauma narratives

A peer-reviewed Journal of Behavioral Medicine study indexed August 22 tested a privacy-preserving local LLM on free-text trauma descriptions from 109 online participants. Each completed an extended Life Events Checklist with up to three narratives and a PTSD Checklist for DSM-5; two clinical psychologists supplied comparison classifications.

Specificity exceeded 85% for most trauma types, and agreement was strongest for frequently reported experiences: sexual-assault classification reached kappa values of 0.68 to 0.74. Clusters built from LLM-derived features differed in total PCL-5 scores, with p=.04.

The result is preliminary rather than clinical deployment: this was a small online self-report study, agreement varied across categories, and the abstract does not identify the model. The data are observational behavioral-health reports, not routine-care RWD. Its practical advance is nevertheless clear—a local model can keep sensitive text within the research environment while converting narratives into structured phenotypes for expert-reviewed analysis.

The claim record is becoming the evidence product

The genuinely new signal is not simply better scientific tool use. Brain Researcher makes the pre-analysis commitment, admissible alternatives, execution trace, evidence links, and bounded conclusion parts of one durable object. That structure lets a null result narrow the next question, a human correction become a future check, and a successor study begin without rewriting the evidentiary history.

The local trauma study points to the adjacent opportunity: keep sensitive health evidence close to its source, make the transformation inspectable, and route uncertainty to domain experts. As agentic evidence systems mature, the most valuable output may be the transparent record of how a claim became supportable—and the exact conditions under which it should travel.
