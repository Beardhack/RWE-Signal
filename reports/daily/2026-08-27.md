# A longitudinal foundation model learned from 1.67 million cancer journeys

**RWE-Signal - August 27, 2026 - Daily edition**

Three new releases show AI-enabled evidence work taking shape around explicit, auditable objects: a patient trajectory, a guideline criterion, and a trial registration.

## A foundation model represented longitudinal oncology evidence

- **Source:** [oFM v1 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24688)
- **Date:** August 25, 2026
- **What changed:** oFM integrates longitudinal clinical records with DNA, RNA, and pathology from 1.67 million cancer patients. Overall-survival AUC reached 0.774 versus 0.563 for curated baseline features, and treatment-benefit ranking improved in 9 of 11 comparative-treatment cohorts.
- **Evidence limits:** This is a vendor-authored living preprint using retrospective proprietary data. It evaluates representation quality, not a validated causal treatment effect or clinical deployment.

## Structured guidance strengthened guideline-appraisal agents

- **Source:** [Peer-reviewed JMIR study](https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e96002)
- **Date:** August 26, 2026
- **What changed:** Two retrieval-augmented agents appraised 227 clinical-practice guidelines. Adding an expert-developed workbook raised agent-human agreement; DeepSeek-R1's ICC increased from 0.613 to 0.709.
- **Evidence limits:** External validation used only six ACL-reconstruction guidelines, and shared-framework agreement is not independent ground truth.

## TrialScout restored links between trials and their results

- **Source:** [Peer-reviewed PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42648652/)
- **Date:** August 26, 2026
- **What changed:** The LLM workflow reached 92.5% sensitivity and 81.2% specificity against human coding, and found result papers for 6,110 of 9,600 completed or terminated trials.
- **Evidence limits:** Manual review showed that the human reference itself contained errors; no perfect gold standard exists.

## The signal

The strongest systems are making evidence objects explicit before asking AI to reason over them. That choice creates a path to reusable representations, structured review, provenance recovery, and human verification at scale.

## Source health

arXiv and JMIR complete primary text were accessible. PubMed supplied TrialScout's peer-reviewed abstract and publication history; the publisher full text did not render publicly. Public LinkedIn indexing remained incomplete, and the ISPE program was inaccessible.
