# Start with people who change decisions

**External RWE Signal Watch — August 4, 2026 — Bootstrap edition**

This inaugural edition establishes the outside-Datavant watchlist and editorial method. It is not a same-day public-web scan and makes no claim that the linked experts posted on August 4.

## Editorial premise

The strongest signal is specific enough to challenge: a concrete method, measured result, explicit limitation, clearly bounded use context, or verifiable regulatory development. The ledger ranks those contributions above generic AI acceleration, vague “complete data” language, and privacy claims that treat tokenization as equivalent to de-identification.

The working model is:

1. Tokenization connects records.
2. Expert Determination evaluates whether a release has very small re-identification risk in context.
3. RWE science determines whether the linked data can support the scientific question.

Frontier AI may coordinate those layers, but it does not eliminate study design, privacy analysis, validation, provenance, or human accountability.

## Daily inclusion threshold

Items score up to 10 points for topic relevance, evidence quality, novelty, external decision value, and timeliness. A score of 6 is required for inclusion; lead items normally score 8 or higher. Promotional framing, avoidable secondhand reporting, unclear commercial interests, and recycled claims are penalized.

No-new-signal days are valid and should remain visible in the archive.
