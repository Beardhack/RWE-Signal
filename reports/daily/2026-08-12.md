# The AI cut estimated review time. It also missed 49% of the search.

**RWE-Signal - August 12, 2026 - Daily edition**

Three peer-reviewed validations found useful automation at the shallow end of evidence work—and brittle performance where completeness and clinical interpretation matter most.

## The fastest search retrieved only 22 of 43 benchmark articles

- **Source:** [Bloudek et al., PharmacoEconomics](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40273-026-01648-7)
- **Date:** July 29, 2026
- **What happened:** An AI-assisted replication of a health-economic systematic review estimated time savings of up to 60% for search and title-and-abstract screening, but its generated search retrieved only 51% of the original PubMed articles. Accuracy exceeded 95% at title-and-abstract screening and fell below 80% at full text.
- **Why it matters:** Search completeness and correct interpretation of economic-model assumptions are not clerical details; they determine the evidence base and can change a model conclusion.
- **Evidence limits:** One commercial platform, one prior review, vendor-affiliated authors, and publisher full text behind a subscription.

## GPT-4o's safety-narrative performance depended on the element

- **Source:** [Warner et al., Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpt.70409)
- **Date:** July 29, 2026
- **What happened:** Across five historical pharmacovigilance signal assessments, GPT-4o extraction produced F1 scores from 0.444 to 1.000 for risk factors and 0.429 to 0.909 for dechallenge and rechallenge.
- **Why it matters:** The broad range makes task-specific human verification part of the evidence architecture, not a temporary concession to model maturity.
- **Evidence limits:** Retrospective proprietary system, all authors affiliated with the sponsor, incomplete denominators in the accessible abstract, and no prospective external validation.

## Two million notes became 2.5 million staging records

- **Source:** [Abhyankar et al., JCO Clinical Cancer Informatics](https://ascopubs.org/doi/abs/10.1200/CCI-25-00388)
- **Date:** July 13, 2026
- **What happened:** A fine-tuned oncology LLM used 700 ground-truth notes, then generated more than 2.5 million staging records for 217,768 patients from more than two million notes at one integrated health system.
- **Why it matters:** The scale is a genuine RWD gain, but relation-level precision does not establish cross-system transportability or show whether errors alter a downstream cohort or result.
- **Evidence limits:** One health system, company-developed model and authors, limited ground truth, and external validation still underway.

## What changed

AI is becoming useful across evidence operations, but the validation target must move downstream. The decisive question is not how much labor a model saves; it is whether missed studies, misread assumptions, or incorrect phenotypes change the evidence decision.

## Source health

Official journal abstracts, DOI records, PubMed, and Europe PMC metadata were accessible for all included studies. Publisher full text required subscriptions for the three articles. Public LinkedIn indexing remained incomplete and was used only for discovery.
