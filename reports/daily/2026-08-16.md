# Agentic validation reached across oncology submissions

**RWE-Signal - August 16, 2026 - Daily edition**

Four new sources show frontier AI scaling evidence quality control, study screening, and outcome ascertainment while preserving explicit verification layers.

## CAVE-Onc connected oncology submission domains

- **Source:** [Yan, PLOS One](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0350376)
- **Date:** August 14, 2026
- **What happened:** A graph-constrained validation system combined 111 declarative shapes with a deterministic LangGraph agent for cross-domain RECIST reasoning.
- **Why it matters:** CAVE detected all 10 tested cross-domain contradiction types that two domain-scoped validators could not express, then transferred most applicable injected checks to two mapped real-trial structures. Code, benchmark data, tests, and audit artifacts are open.
- **Evidence limits:** The headline result is construction validation on injected contradictions, not an estimate of error prevalence in raw regulatory submissions.

## ScreenAgent made a 201,064-record evidence search reviewable

- **Source:** [Dobin et al., medRxiv v1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.12.26360335v1)
- **Date:** August 14, 2026
- **What happened:** A cascade screening agent identified 43 of 44 eligible studies in a prospective suicide-prevention meta-analysis and removed 99.4% of records from human review.
- **Why it matters:** Two external review validations also exceeded 95% sensitivity while reducing review workload by more than 97%, with humans retaining final inclusion authority.
- **Evidence limits:** This is a preprint; external results were cross-validated after prompt tuning, and code and data are available only on request.

## LLM-assisted chart review expanded bleeding-event capture

- **Source:** [Reuland et al., Transfusion Clinique et Biologique](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42600978/)
- **Date:** August 14, 2026
- **What happened:** GPT-4o-mini screened 36,490 ICU notes and identified 647 of 654 adjudicated true bleeding events, including 588 not captured in the initial manual review.
- **Why it matters:** The workflow turned high-recall model screening plus expert adjudication into a substantially richer event dataset.
- **Evidence limits:** The single-centre study included 149 patients, and site and severity classification remained less accurate than event detection.

## An EMR-integrated LLM validated cardiovascular outcomes across three sites

- **Source:** [Ibrahim et al., BMJ Open](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-116133)
- **Date:** August 13, 2026
- **What happened:** A zero-shot LLM was tested against manual adjudication for stroke, myocardial infarction, heart failure, and MACE in 3,684 patients.
- **Why it matters:** The LLM achieved the highest AUC in seven of eight outcome-cohort comparisons and significantly improved stroke and MACE retrieval over ICD codes in the TAVR cohort.
- **Evidence limits:** The three sites belonged to one health system, one model configuration was tested, and formal timing and hybrid retrieval were not evaluated.

## What changed

The new pattern is verified scale: graphs, structured agents, cascades, prespecified output rules, and expert adjudication are making larger evidence workflows both feasible and inspectable.
