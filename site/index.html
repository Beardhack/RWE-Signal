The editorial premise

High signal is specific enough to be challenged

The strongest people in this field publish concrete methods, measured results, explicit limitations, and well-bounded claims. This ledger ranks those contributions above generic AI acceleration, vague “complete data” language, or privacy claims that collapse tokenization and de-identification into the same concept.

This inaugural edition establishes the watchlist; it is not presented as a same-day web scan. Future daily editions will use a 48-hour post window, a 14-day publication and regulatory window, persistent deduplication, and a score that rewards primary evidence, novelty, and practical decision value.

The core model: tokenization connects records; Expert Determination evaluates whether a release has very small re-identification risk; RWE methods determine whether the linked data can support the scientific question. Frontier AI may coordinate all three, but it replaces none of them.

What will make the ledger

An item must score at least 6/10 across relevance, evidence quality, novelty, external value, and timeliness. Lead items normally score 8 or higher. Unsupported promotion, avoidable secondhand reporting, recycled claims, and unclear commercial interests are penalized.

The result can be short. “No new high-signal items” is a valid edition when the public web is quiet or key sources cannot be verified.
