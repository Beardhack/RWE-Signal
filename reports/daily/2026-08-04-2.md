# The LLM stops at the schema

**RWE-Signal &mdash; August 4, 2026 &mdash; Second edition**

[THESEUS](https://academic.oup.com/jamiaopen/article/9/4/ooag131/8729191) gives an LLM a deliberately narrow job inside target-trial emulation. The model converts natural-language study descriptions into a reviewable JSON specification; deterministic rules then generate Strategus R code for an OMOP environment.

Eight models were evaluated on 30 studies. Primary-analysis specification accuracy ranged from 0.93 to 0.97 for OHDSI studies and from 0.82 to 0.95 outside OHDSI. That is direct evidence for protocol standardization, not for a complete autonomous study.

The boundary matters. THESEUS covers four core specification groups; unsupported analytic choices were excluded, cohort definition remains separate, and the paper did not test analyst time savings or end-to-end causal validity. Adjacent causal-agent research supports the same architecture: constrain the model, execute with deterministic tools, preserve diagnostics, and let a human stop the workflow.

The new signal is a practical division of labor for AI-enabled RWE. Language models interpret protocol prose. Structured artifacts and deterministic software carry the analysis forward.
