# A clean balance can hide a broken trial

**RWE-Signal — August 4, 2026 — Second edition**

Two July target-trial studies expose confounding that survived conventional diagnostics and deep-learning adjustment, while new agentic tools show why guardrails—not autonomy—are the useful frontier.

An [iScience diagnostic study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13355737/) produced opposite estimates from two pre-specified exposure definitions in the same 42,883-patient cohort. Initial fluid assignment suggested benefit (OR 0.49), while 48-hour dominant fluid suggested harm (OR 2.51), even after weighting reduced the maximum standardized mean difference to 0.048. The hidden clue was an 89.4% switching rate shaped by local ICU workflow.

A separate [Nature Communications heart-failure study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74999-6) found that propensity-score matching, inverse weighting, targeted maximum likelihood, and a Transformer all failed to reproduce positive- and negative-control randomized benchmarks. The methods recovered truth in semi-synthetic data when confounders were observed. The real-world failure was not insufficient model flexibility; it was information the comparison did not capture.

The most credible agentic work is converging on the same answer. The [oci-agent preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22443) uses deterministic notebooks, design diagnostics, a critic, and human certification; prompt-only output was uncorrelated with ground truth on a selected ten-dataset test, while the scaffolded workflow recovered nine. [THESEUS](https://academic.oup.com/jamiaopen/article/9/4/ooag131/8729191) maps protocol text into a reviewable schema before deterministic Strategus code generation, reaching primary-analysis standardization accuracy of 0.93–0.97 on OHDSI studies and 0.82–0.95 on non-OHDSI studies.

Privacy is moving toward the same contextual standard. The [EDPB's draft anonymisation guidelines](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/public-consultations/guidelines-022026-on-anonymisation_en) test record isolation, linkage, and inference from the perspective of relevant recipients. A [Spanish clinical-text study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42458625/) found generic and prompt-based approaches at macro F1 0.14–0.50, versus up to 0.956 after task-specific fine-tuning. That is performance evidence, not a re-identification-risk determination.

The through-line is diagnostic discipline. A model can execute a method; it cannot make missing confounding, unstable exposure definitions, or an underspecified release environment disappear.
