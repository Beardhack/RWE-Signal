# Brain Researcher made agentic neuroimaging auditable

**RWE-Signal - August 23, 2026 - Daily edition**

A new Stanford-led research harness raised first-action tool routing across seven frontier models from 23.3% to 93.6%, then bound real neuroimaging analyses to frozen plans, versioned execution, provenance, and six reviewable claim states. A peer-reviewed local-LLM study separately structured trauma narratives without sending sensitive text to a cloud service.

## The agent produced an evidence object

- **Sources:** [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19902), [open agent kit](https://github.com/brain-researcher/brain-researcher-agent-kit/tree/v0.3.0), and [archived release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21937526)
- **Date:** August 20, 2026
- **What happened:** Brain Researcher combined a tool registry, provenance graph, MCP server, version-pinned executors, commitment cards, claim cards, and an audit bundle. Across 60 tasks and seven models, correct first routing rose from 23.3% to 93.6%; verified grounding rose from 4.6% to 22.0% in a separate benchmark.
- **Why it matters:** The completed unit is a reopenable research episode linking the question, approved plan, tool calls, evidence, artifacts, checks, and bounded claim—not just a generated answer.
- **Evidence limits:** This is a preprint. The paired comparison measures the whole harness, grounding remains incomplete, and expert inspection remains essential.

## Multiverse analysis became part of claim governance

- **Source:** [full preprint HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19902v1)
- **Date:** August 20, 2026
- **What happened:** The system expanded collaborator questions into multiverse analyses on real neuroimaging cohorts, prevented weak exploratory findings from being promoted as confirmatory, and froze successor analyses before execution. Claims were classified as accepted, qualified, revised, blocked, rejected, or deferred.
- **Why it matters:** Analytic alternatives, null results, and human review become durable parts of the evidence record and can safely shape the next study.
- **Evidence limits:** A human reviewer caught a sign-blind scoring error missed by automation; the self-evolving comparison was descriptive rather than controlled.

## A local LLM structured sensitive trauma narratives

- **Source:** [Journal of Behavioral Medicine record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42631898/)
- **Date:** August 22, 2026
- **What happened:** In 109 online participants, a locally executed LLM extracted trauma types from free-text self-reports and was compared with two clinical psychologists. Specificity exceeded 85% for most types; sexual-assault agreement reached kappa 0.68-0.74.
- **Why it matters:** Local inference offers a privacy-preserving path from sensitive narrative data to analyzable behavioral-health phenotypes.
- **Evidence limits:** This is a small preliminary online self-report study, not routine-care RWD or clinical deployment, and agreement varied by trauma type.

## The signal

The new signal is the claim record as infrastructure. Brain Researcher shows how agents can make scientific commitments, tool use, provenance, alternative specifications, and human decisions inspectable in one durable object. Local language models extend the same design logic to sensitive phenotyping: keep the evidence close, make the transformation visible, and preserve a clear path for expert review.
