# FedEHR-Agents let hospitals share modeling intelligence without pooling patient records

**RWE-Signal - August 31, 2026 - Monday edition**

A new preprint moves federated clinical AI beyond model-weight exchange: hospital agents share evidence-backed preprocessing and modeling experience while patient-level EHR data, local models, and historical memory stay local.

## Evidence-guided federation improved four EHR tasks

- **Source:** [FedEHR-Agents arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27856)
- **Date:** August 28, 2026
- **What changed:** On eICU hospital partitions, FedEHR-Agents outperformed the same agent working locally on 48-hour mortality, acute respiratory failure, prolonged length of stay, and sepsis prediction. Relative AUPRC gains ranged from 8.3% to 17.9%, and evidence-guided aggregation beat simple prompt averaging on all four tasks.
- **Evidence limits:** This is a non-peer-reviewed retrospective benchmark, not a live multi-health-system deployment. Shared prompts and modeling statistics keep raw records local but do not yet provide a formal privacy guarantee.

## The signal

The important unit of collaboration is no longer only a model parameter. FedEHR-Agents shows that agents can exchange a structured, evaluable playbook for how to clean EHRs, select features, build models, and improve from feedback. The next unlock is to pair that reusable modeling intelligence with formal privacy protection and prospective, cross-system validation.
