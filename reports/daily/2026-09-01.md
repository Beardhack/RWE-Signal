# An LLM turned public flu posts into an early vaccine-effectiveness signal

**RWE-Signal - September 1, 2026 - Tuesday edition**

Five new studies show language models moving into the working infrastructure of evidence: estimating vaccine effectiveness, recovering phenotypes from clinical text, auditing trial corpora, appraising RWE bias, and testing agents on realistic primary-care workflows.

## Public posts became an epidemiologic pipeline

- **Source:** [medRxiv v1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.28.26361670v1)
- **Date:** August 31, 2026
- **What changed:** LLaMA-3-70B-Instruct classified vaccination and influenza-test status from public posts, then a test-negative-like design estimated 43% vaccine effectiveness for 2025-26 (95% CI 14%-62%). The cumulative estimate entered and stayed within the CDC interim range roughly nine weeks before the interim report.
- **Evidence limits:** This is an unadjusted, non-peer-reviewed estimate with small test-negative groups and no independent current-season validation of the classifier. The accompanying repository begins from classifier output rather than reproducing the upstream collection and prompting pipeline.

## Clinical text exposed hidden emergency-department phenotypes

- **Source:** [medRxiv v1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.26.26361443v1)
- **Date:** August 31, 2026
- **What changed:** Against an adjudicated 2,256-attendance validation set, a locally deployed LLM matched or exceeded clinician balanced accuracy for alcohol-, drug-, and self-harm involvement. Applied to 105,096 annual attendances, it identified substantially more domain involvement than coded data alone.
- **Evidence limits:** The study is a single-site UK preprint and does not yet establish transportability.

## Model-assisted audit expanded a cancer-screening evidence base

- **Source:** [medRxiv v1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.29.26361733v1)
- **Date:** August 31, 2026
- **What changed:** An LLM prioritized reports for blinded human reassessment, helping add eight compatible comparisons to a 41-comparison review. The expanded evidence base reduced the overall trial-level correlation between late-stage incidence and cancer-specific mortality from 0.73 to 0.59.
- **Evidence limits:** Humans retained every eligibility, reconciliation, endpoint, and extraction decision. This is a preprint and does not validate one universal screening surrogate.

## RWE appraisal got a 40-model benchmark

- **Source:** [PubMed 42640121](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42640121/)
- **Date:** September 2026 issue
- **What changed:** Forty LLMs appraised ten published RWE studies across 28 APPRAISE items. Best accuracy reached 66.1% and best macro-F1 reached 66.9%, while model review time was 0.80-17.22 minutes per study versus 61.05 minutes for humans.
- **Evidence limits:** The peer-reviewed benchmark uses ten studies and one appraisal framework; results varied materially by model and repeated-run reproducibility was not tested.

## A realistic primary-care benchmark widened the clinical-agent action space

- **Source:** [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30188)
- **Date:** August 31, 2026
- **What changed:** GPAgentBench-2K evaluates 16 LLMs on a safety-constrained, six-action workflow built from expert-validated records of real-world GP encounters. A constrained optimization baseline improved on unconstrained reinforcement learning.
- **Evidence limits:** This is a non-peer-reviewed benchmark, not a clinical deployment. Even the strongest diagnostic models violated safety constraints in more than half of high-risk cases, making abstention and workflow constraints a central development target.

## The signal

The new signal is not one fully autonomous evidence system. It is a practical architecture recurring across five independent studies: let language models find, classify, prioritize, or structure evidence at scale; preserve an explicit statistical design, adjudicated reference, or expert decision layer; and publish enough provenance to test the handoff. That is a credible route from promising automation to evidence infrastructure.

## Source health

medRxiv, PubMed, arXiv, Crossref, and the influenza analysis repository were accessible. The JMCP publisher page was not consistently discoverable, and medRxiv's direct HTML route intermittently failed, but primary abstracts, metadata, and the influenza PDF remained available. Public LinkedIn indexing remained incomplete and was used only for discovery.
