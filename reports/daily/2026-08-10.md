# The AI found 3,460 contradictions. It could not say how many were real.

**RWE-Signal - August 10, 2026 - Daily edition**

An LLM pipeline flagged possible inconsistencies in 69.7% of 3,000 real discharge summaries, but without a clinician-labeled corpus it could not measure precision or sensitivity.

## The detector produced a review queue, not a verdict

- **Source:** [Lu et al., arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22954)
- **Date:** July 24, 2026
- **What happened:** Gemini 2.5 Pro generated possible contradictions from MIMIC-IV discharge summaries and Gemini 2.5 Flash verified them against context. The pipeline returned 3,460 candidate pairs across 3,000 notes.
- **Why it matters:** Documentation conflicts can contaminate AI-derived phenotypes and research variables, but a high flag rate cannot be treated as an error prevalence estimate.
- **Evidence limits:** Formative preprint; only a subset received expert review; no clinician-labeled reference corpus, precision, or sensitivity.

## The evidence-review trial favored the LLM without proving a time saving

- **Source:** [Ishikane et al., Open Forum Infectious Diseases](https://academic.oup.com/ofid/article/13/7/ofag401/8736362)
- **Date:** July 23, 2026
- **What happened:** Five experienced reviewers completed 20 randomized crossover tasks with an o3-generated structured summary or manual PDF review. Adjusted completion time was 7.9 minutes faster with the LLM, but the 95% confidence interval crossed zero.
- **Why it matters:** The trial measured researcher time in a controlled design rather than relying on model accuracy alone.
- **Evidence limits:** Five reviewers, six mpox-related papers, 20 tasks, a single expert accuracy judge, no repeated-generation stability test, and no evaluation of searching or screening.

## What changed

AI systems are producing useful intermediate artifacts faster than researchers can validate the final judgments. The practical standard is triage plus audit: candidate volume is not precision, and a favorable point estimate is not a demonstrated effect.

## Source health

arXiv, Oxford Academic, Europe PMC, PubMed, FDA, ISPOR, and institutional pages were accessible. A medRxiv full-text route was inconsistent during assessment of an excluded candidate, and public LinkedIn indexing remained incomplete. Included claims were verified from accessible primary full text; social indexing was used only for discovery.
