# HealthFlow turned EHR analysis into a self-evolving multi-agent system

**RWE-Signal - August 28, 2026 - Friday edition**

Three peer-reviewed systems advance AI-enabled real-world evidence from bounded extraction tasks toward end-to-end, evaluable analytical workflows.

## HealthFlow made EHR analysis a governed agent loop

- **Sources:** [npj Digital Medicine article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03097-0) and [open repository](https://github.com/yhzhu99/HealthFlow)
- **Date:** August 17, 2026
- **What changed:** HealthFlow plans, executes, evaluates, repairs, and retains governed experience across 100 benchmark EHR-analysis tasks. It scored 4.01 versus 3.76 for the strongest baseline on EHRFlowBench and 63.05% versus 50.10% on MedAgentBoard.
- **Evidence limits:** The benchmark partly uses an LLM judge, the MIMIC input is the public demo, and the hospital pilot was qualitative and offline rather than prospective.

## An LLM reconstructed CAR-T safety timelines

- **Source:** [peer-reviewed PLOS Digital Health record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42658846/)
- **Date:** August 27, 2026
- **What changed:** GPT-4 structured adverse-event type, timing, grade, and intervention from 4,762 notes covering 293 patients. Focused grading accuracy reached 0.97 for cytokine-release syndrome and 0.91 for neurotoxicity.
- **Evidence limits:** Dual review covered 50 notes from one center; broad adverse-event attributes were harder and documentation gaps were common.

## Local LLMs structured symptom surveillance data

- **Source:** [peer-reviewed JMIR article](https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e98580)
- **Date:** August 26, 2026
- **What changed:** Twelve locally deployed open-source LLMs were tested on chief complaints sampled from a citywide EHR platform. Qwen3-8B reached macro F1 0.89, and Qwen3-1.7B reached 0.85.
- **Evidence limits:** The evaluation used 1,000 records from one city and one EHR field, not the platform's full 167.6 million records or a live surveillance deployment.

## The weekly signal

This week's strongest systems made evidence inspectable. The next unlock is to preserve that architecture while testing it across institutions and prospective evidence decisions.
