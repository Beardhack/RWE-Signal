# FDA put an AI program on a real-world evidence contract.

**RWE-Signal - August 13, 2026 - Daily edition**

Dexcom's first-in-pilot glucose program will enter Medicare-linked chronic-care delivery while its maker collects outcome data. That is a consequential change in evidence infrastructure, not yet proof that the program works.

## The AI and the evidence plan now share a pathway

- **Source:** [FDA participant announcement](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-announces-first-participant-selected-tempo-digital-health-devices-pilot)
- **Date:** July 22, 2026
- **What happened:** FDA selected Dexcom as the first participant in its TEMPO digital-health pilot. The program will combine glucose-sensor data with nutrition, activity, sleep, and stress data to provide AI-enabled insights while the manufacturer collects, monitors, and reports RWD tied to its intended uses.
- **Why it matters:** FDA and CMS are coupling regulatory oversight, Medicare-linked access, outcome-aligned payment, and prospective RWE generation in one operating model.
- **Evidence limits:** No participant-level protocol, sample size, comparator, analysis plan, monitoring cadence, algorithm-change policy, or decision threshold was disclosed. No clinical result has been reported.

## What changed

The important innovation is the evidence contract around the AI. The pilot may show whether personalized glucose insights change screening or glycemic outcomes in routine use, but the credibility of that answer will depend on the design details that remain private or unfinished.

## Source health

FDA, CMS, Dexcom, publication indexes, preprint servers, and institutional frontier-health-AI pages were accessible. Public LinkedIn indexing remained incomplete and was used only for discovery.
