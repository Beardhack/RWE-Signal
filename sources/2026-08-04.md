## Evidence reviewed

### Context only: Hisashi Noma — TTE tutorial and R package

- Direct sources: [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01625), [public code and package documentation](https://github.com/nomahi/TTE)
- Date: August 3, 2026
- Internal score: 9/10 for the preprint; 8/10 for the implementation repository
- Establishes: a self-contained workflow for target-trial protocols, intention-to-treat and per-protocol estimands, longitudinal data construction, treatment and censoring weights, weight diagnostics, pooled discrete-time survival models, standardization, competing risks, weighted curves, and individual-cluster bootstrap.
- Implementation detail: TTE 1.1.1 is the first CRAN release. The repository includes fully synthetic SGLT2i-versus-DPP-4i and ARB-versus-CCB examples and public scripts.
- Limits: the paper is a single-author preprint; the worked examples are synthetic; the package explicitly does not identify a causal effect automatically. Applied users still have to defend eligibility, treatment strategies, time zero, outcomes, confounding control, assumptions, and estimands.

Context: the [May 2026 BMJ systematic review and meta-analysis](https://www.bmj.com/content/393/bmj-2025-086810) covered 107 target-trial-emulation/RCT pairs. It reported 79% standardized-difference agreement, 77% estimate agreement, Pearson correlation 0.59, and 74% of estimates within 20% of the paired trial result. Concordance was weaker when key trial features, populations, treatment timing, and outcomes could not be closely emulated. This older source is used as context, not presented as new today.

### Context only: FDA — FY2025 CDER RWE submission ledger

- Direct source: [FDA CDER RWE submissions](https://www.fda.gov/science-research/real-world-evidence/real-world-evidence-submissions-center-drug-evaluation-and-research)
- Date: current page reporting FY2023–FY2025
- Internal score: 9/10
- Establishes: protocols containing RWD rose from 11 in FY2024 to 31 in FY2025; NDA/BLA submissions containing RWE rose from 1 to 10. The FY2025 protocol set included 25 non-interventional studies, five externally controlled trials, and one randomized trial. Fifteen protocols focused on effectiveness and 16 on safety.
- Regulatory outcome detail: among the ten FY2025 NDA/BLA submissions, FDA reports four approvals where RWE contributed to the decision, one approval where it did not, four complete responses, and one refusal to file. The four contributing approvals were Acetadote, Onapgo, Isturisa, and Gamifant.
- Limits: FDA says the page does not include every CDER submission containing RWD analyses; studies may appear in multiple categories; an inclusion count is not an acceptance rate or quality score.

### Context only: Lee et al. — data continuity in linked EHR and claims data

- Direct source: [JAMIA Open article](https://academic.oup.com/jamiaopen/article/9/4/ooag124/8733482)
- Date: published July 13, 2026; appears in the August 2026 issue
- Internal score: 8/10
- Authors: Yao An Lee, Tiange Tang, Yu Huang, Jiang Bian, Lizheng Shi, and Jingchuan Guo
- Establishes: linked OneFlorida+ EHR–Florida Medicaid claims data were used for development and linked REACHnet EHR–Louisiana Blue claims for external validation. Misclassification across 42 clinical variables declined as EHR continuity increased. A study-specific HEPS threshold near 30% separated higher-continuity records; the best development XGBoost model reached AUROC 0.77.
- Equity detail: the original complete-predictor model had a false-negative rate of 0.60 for Hispanic patients versus 0.35 for non-Hispanic White patients; after adversarial debiasing these were 0.46 and 0.50. The authors chose false-negative rate because misclassifying a truly high-continuity patient as low continuity can inappropriately exclude that person from research.
- Limits: continuity was defined against linked claims in two networks, the 30% threshold is study-specific, and selecting for observable patients can change cohort composition even when prediction fairness improves.

Applied illustration: [Cajiao Garcia et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42288045/) linked the Netherlands Cancer Registry with nationwide pathology reports. In stage IV non-squamous NSCLC, RET testing rose from 22.6% in 2017 to 31.8% in 2019 while the testing mix shifted from FISH toward RNA-based methods; observed RET-fusion prevalence fell from 1.3% to 0.7%. The authors concluded that published 1–2% prevalence estimates may be high. The August issue date makes this current context, though the cohorts themselves are historical and the result is setting-specific.

### 4. Li et al. and JBI — agentic RWE moves to lifecycle evaluation

- `ai_rwe_intersection`: passes. The same primary sources materially concern agentic or multi-agent AI and named RWD/RWE lifecycle tasks, evaluation, and governance.

- Direct sources: [Nature Communications paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74501-2), [JBI special-issue call](https://www.sciencedirect.com/special-issue/334546/agentic-ai-for-real-world-evidence-life-cycle-support-scaling-trustworthy-evaluation-of-treatment-safety-and-effectiveness)
- Dates: Nature paper published July 7, 2026; JBI call is active with a November 15, 2026 deadline and appears as an August editorial
- Internal scores: 8/10 and 7/10
- Establishes: EmulatRx divides work among Supervisor, Trialist, Informatician, Clinician, and Statistician agents. It was evaluated on 20 trials across MIMIC-IV and INSIGHT. On 266 manually annotated eligibility concepts, GPT-4o achieved 98.9% recall and 96.7% precision. The system stores workflow state and uses fixed seeds for downstream tools.
- Limits: several causal-estimation evaluations used synthetic data, the trial set was small, and clinician assessment involved three experts. This is evidence of a promising research system, not regulatory validation or proof of autonomous study credibility.
- Field signal: the JBI call defines agentic RWE across acquisition, harmonization, phenotyping, protocol work, causal critique, effect estimation, provenance, monitoring, governance, and deployment. It explicitly places isolated tool-augmented agents outside scope. The call is an editorial agenda, not empirical evidence.

## What changed

FDA's FY2025 totals show a larger regulatory pipeline, but its outcomes remain mixed.
