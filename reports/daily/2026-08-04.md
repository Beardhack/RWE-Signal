# Five agents enter the RWE lifecycle

**RWE-Signal &mdash; August 4, 2026 &mdash; Daily edition**

Agentic RWE became a systems claim. [EmulatRx](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74501-2) coordinates a Supervisor, Trialist, Informatician, Clinician, and Statistician across trial retrieval, protocol parsing, cohort construction, clinical review, and causal analysis.

The strongest result is task-specific. Across 20 trials in MIMIC-IV and INSIGHT, GPT-4o reached 98.9% recall and 96.7% precision on 266 manually annotated eligibility concepts. Some causal-effect tests still used synthetic cohorts of 1,000 subjects, and clinician assessment involved three experts. The paper demonstrates an integrated AI-plus-RWE architecture; it does not establish autonomous, decision-grade evidence generation.

An active [Journal of Biomedical Informatics special issue](https://www.sciencedirect.com/special-issue/334546/agentic-ai-for-real-world-evidence-life-cycle-support-scaling-trustworthy-evaluation-of-treatment-safety-and-effectiveness) points in the same direction. It asks the field to evaluate full evidence lifecycles, provenance, causal critique, governance, and safe deployment rather than isolated tool calls.

The through-line is orchestration with visible limits. Specialized agents can make an RWE production line more inspectable; they do not make its scientific judgments disappear.
