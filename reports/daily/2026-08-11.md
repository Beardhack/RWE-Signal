# The LLM built a 17,223-patient cohort. The guideline's middle still collapsed.

**RWE-Signal - August 11, 2026 - Daily edition**

An LLM-enabled three-system study validated a new pulmonary-embolism framework at the extremes, but not across the intermediate band where many treatment decisions sit.

## The cohort scaled. The middle categories did not separate.

- **Source:** [Alwakeel et al., medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.06.26359865v1)
- **Date:** August 10, 2026
- **What happened:** An independently evaluated LLM abstraction framework converted routine CTPA reports into imaging phenotypes and helped assemble 17,223 confirmed PE patients across Duke, Stanford, and Beth Israel Deaconess. Thirty-day mortality rose from 1.5% in category B to 31.9% in E, but the detailed intermediate strata were nonmonotonic and did not improve discrimination over a collapsed middle band.
- **Why it matters:** The study demonstrates a real downstream RWE use of LLM-derived variables while showing that cohort scale cannot validate a clinical taxonomy whose decision-relevant middle remains unstable.
- **Evidence limits:** Retrospective preprint; missing tests counted as criteria not met; imaging-extraction errors can propagate; treatment confounding and incomplete external-cohort data remain.

## A 96.4% accuracy headline hid a 57.6% precision problem

- **Source:** [Bhatt et al., medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.05.26359802v1)
- **Date:** August 10, 2026
- **What happened:** Llama 3.3 70B identified PCI reports with 96.4% accuracy across three hospitals, but positive predictive value fell to 57.6% for the harder complex-PCI phenotype. Most report-level false positives involved diagnostic IVUS- or FFR-only procedures mistaken for intervention.
- **Why it matters:** High document-level accuracy does not guarantee a research-ready phenotype. Unreviewed false positives could alter cohort composition and downstream comparative-effectiveness estimates.
- **Evidence limits:** Retrospective preprint from one health system; no prospective deployment; substantial site heterogeneity; data and code unavailable under privacy and IRB restrictions.

## What changed

LLM-derived variables are moving into real observational cohorts. The decisive validation question is now whether extraction errors, missingness, and site differences change the research conclusion—not merely whether the model can parse a note.

## Source health

Both medRxiv preprints and the supporting peer-reviewed abstraction evaluation were accessible. Europe PMC, PubMed, FDA, ISPOR, and institutional pages were available. Public LinkedIn indexing remained incomplete and was used only for discovery.
