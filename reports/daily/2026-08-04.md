# Two architectures put guardrails around AI for RWE

**RWE-Signal — August 4, 2026 — Daily edition**

Two peer-reviewed July papers pass the AI-plus-RWE intersection gate and point to the same operating principle: AI is most credible in an evidence workflow when its role, handoffs, and stopping points are explicit.

## 1. EmulatRx coordinates five agents across trial emulation

- **Source:** [Li et al., Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74501-2)
- **Date:** July 7, 2026
- **Topic:** Multi-agent systems for RWD/RWE
- **What happened:** A Supervisor coordinates Trialist, Informatician, Clinician, and Statistician agents across protocol interpretation, cohort construction, clinical review, causal analysis, and synthesis. The system was evaluated on 20 trials across MIMIC-IV and INSIGHT.
- **Why it matters:** It treats the RWE lifecycle—not a single generated output—as the unit of automation. On 266 annotated eligibility concepts, GPT-4o reached 98.9% recall and 96.7% precision; one emulation gained 33 eligible patients from notes beyond structured EHR retrieval.
- **Evidence limits:** Those are task-level results. Some causal evaluations used synthetic cohorts, clinician assessment involved three experts, and the full workflow was not shown to produce autonomous decision-grade evidence.
- **Practical follow-up:** Which agent handoffs change the estimand or study population, and can an independent reviewer reconstruct each change from stored state?

## 2. THESEUS separates LLM interpretation from deterministic code generation

- **Source:** [Kim et al., JAMIA Open](https://academic.oup.com/jamiaopen/article/9/4/ooag131/8729191)
- **Date:** July 9, 2026
- **Topic:** LLM-assisted target-trial emulation
- **What happened:** An LLM maps natural-language target-trial descriptions into a structured JSON specification. A human reviews field-level changes before deterministic rules generate Strategus R code.
- **Why it matters:** It limits model discretion to language interpretation and creates a reviewable intermediate artifact before executable analysis exists. Across eight models and 30 studies, primary-analysis accuracy ranged from 0.93–0.97 for OHDSI studies and 0.82–0.95 for non-OHDSI studies.
- **Evidence limits:** The system covers four specification groups, excludes unsupported choices, leaves cohort definition outside scope, and was not evaluated for time saved or end-to-end scientific validity.
- **Practical follow-up:** How stable are the structured specifications across institutions, reporting styles, and models, and which field-level errors have the greatest causal consequence?

## What changed

Architecture is becoming part of the evidence. EmulatRx makes specialized agent handoffs inspectable; THESEUS narrows the model's job and hands execution to deterministic software. Neither removes the need to defend cohort fitness, estimands, confounding control, endpoint validity, and transportability.
