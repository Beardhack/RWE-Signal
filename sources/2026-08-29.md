## High-interest candidates not published

### Multi-agent discharge-readiness assessment

- Primary source: [Computers in Biology and Medicine: Update DOI 10.1016/j.cmpbup.2026.100261](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmpbup.2026.100261)
- First public date: June 26, 2026; Crossref metadata was redeposited August 28.
- Evidence: five specialist agents, deterministic claim verification, and a reliability-aware arbiter were evaluated on 200 MIMIC-IV cases. The best GPT-4.1 configuration reported F1 0.620, sensitivity 61.3%, and specificity 75.8%.
- `ai_rwe_intersection`: **passes** as an agentic workflow that reasons over and verifies real-world EHR evidence.
- Decision: not eligible because the first public record falls outside the discovery window and the August 28 metadata deposit contains no new scientific version, dataset, result, or decision-relevant change.

### LLM medical chart abstraction for stroke registries

- Primary source: [PubMed PMID 41720514](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720514/); DOI `10.3174/ajnr.A9251`.
- First public date: February 20, 2026.
- Evidence: 22 open-source LLMs extracted eight variables from 2,416 thrombectomy reports across three hospitals. LLaMA3.3-70B reached 94.8% overall accuracy and outperformed nonexpert annotations on seven of eight variables under expert adjudication.
- `ai_rwe_intersection`: **passes** as an LLM workflow structuring real-world stroke records for retrospective research and registry curation.
- Decision: rejected as an older source resurfacing through current PubMed indexing; no material new version was found.
